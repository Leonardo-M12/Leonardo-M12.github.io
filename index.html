I'm Leonardo, and I'm a third-year Applied Mathematics major at Illinois Tech in Chicago. My hometown is Chitré, Panama.

Broadly, my interests lie in discrete mathematics and operations research, especially graph theory, discrete optimization, and stochastic modeling. I'm interested in optimization problems where human factors are as important as efficiency metrics. I'm also interested in how machine learning methods address modeling and data challenges that traditional methods struggle with.

I'm also involved with mathematical Olympiads. I'm a former contestant for the Panama IMO team, and I teach contest math to students in the Panamanian training program.

I currently serve as president of the Illinois Tech SIAM student chapter, where I lead several initiatives for engaging students with math and problem-solving, such as organizing trips to the Argonne National Lab or conducting our own integration bee.

As a fun fact, my Erdős number is 3.
